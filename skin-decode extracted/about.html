ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why Skin Decode?

Ingredient-first by design.

Skin Decode is an educational concept that helps users understand skincare ingredients based on skin type and concerns. It intentionally focuses on ingredients rather than recommending particular products or brands.

How it works

01

Assess

Tell us about your skin.

02

Decode

Match concerns with relevant ingredients.

03

Learn

Read about each ingredient before deciding what suits you.

Safety note

Information on Skin Decode is general educational information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Skin can react differently to ingredients. Patch testing and professional guidance may be appropriate, particularly for sensitive or persistent concerns.
